Sliding filament model
Excitation

Calcium stimulates the binding sites on actin to open once troponin shifts tropomyosin. The action potential travels down the T-tubules and the sarcoplasmic reticulum releases Ca²⁺.

Cross-bridge cycle

ATP hydrolysis cocks the myosin head; it binds actin, the power stroke pulls the thin filament toward the M line, and a new ATP releases the head.

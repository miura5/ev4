What changed from v3

Each item from the independent review of v3 and Matt’s answers, what v4 does, and how it was checked. v1, v2 and v3 stay untouched on their own canvases.

Nav icons are neutralAnswer 1Kept neutral, per D-270. Checked: the sidebar and tab-bar icons use the quiet text colour on every board.
Weak spots in Study, “To review · n” at the topAnswer 2The Weak spots view opens with “To review · 3” above the open ones, and the line is absent at zero. Checked by clicking Not now, Undo and Decline at all four widths: the count and rows follow.
No triage mid-quiz; one line at the endAnswer 3A miss is proposed quietly. The results card carries one line, “2 misses are proposed as weak spots”, with Accept all and Review. Each proposed row shows her suggested objective, not the question, with Accept, Join, Not now and Decline. Join is greyed out when there is no open weak spot under that objective. Checked: across 5 answers × 8 runs there was no Accept button before the results card.
+ New makes a task or a noteAnswer 4The menu has two items, Task for Today and Note. Checked at 390 and 1440: those are the only two.
Check-in drawn; the rest keptAnswer 5The daily check-in (energy 1 to 5 and her advice) is on Conversation; picking 1 changes her advice (clicked at all widths). It hides during a session. The prep check, the Sunday review card, the Deferred and Taken off lines, and the Ship it card are on the Kept board.
Exam plan checklistAnswer 6Named on the Kept board: Study’s Plan tab, unchanged (D-249).
Phone done-checkboxFixThe green fill sits in the 18 px box, and the 44 px tap area is transparent. Checked at 390 on every board with a ticked item: background is rgba(0,0,0,0), and no checkbox’s box overlaps its item’s name (0 overlaps in 32 phone runs).
Tag colours to D-270FixGraded, Class, Workout and the session badge are neutral. Session and the study tags are violet, and Note is blue. Graded turns red only when due today or tomorrow, and none is in this demo. Sidebar and Coming-up dots follow the same rule. Checked: Graded and Class render as the quiet colour on Today, the sidebar and Coming up.
Graded count from the datesFixHer week line and the “What’s due this week?” reply both say three: the lab report draft on Monday, the fluid balance quiz on Tuesday and the renal quiz on Thursday. They are computed from the dates. Checked: the week shows the same three chips.
44 px at 390FixMeasured at 390: Progress bars 47 × 149; Record segments 44 × 44 at the smallest (“All” in the filter); New task estimate segments 44 × 44 at the smallest (“15”, “30”, “45”). An automatic sweep of every visible control on all 16 boards at 390, light and dark, found none under 44 px.
4.5:1 for “Enter” and the placeholderFixMeasured: the Enter key label is 4.91:1 in light and 7.46:1 in dark (6.11 and 8.99 on hover), at full opacity. The dialog’s title placeholder is 5.16:1 in both themes. A sweep of every visible text on all 16 boards at 4 widths in 2 themes found nothing under 4.5:1.
The New dialogFixIt is marked aria-modal. Checked: 20 Tab presses and 20 Shift+Tab presses never left the dialog. Esc closes it and returns focus to New, at 390 and 1440. Esc on the New menu also returns focus to New.
Breakpoints to D-225FixThe tab bar shows below 600 px. From 600 to 1199 px it is the sidebar and the pane, with the panel’s content below the pane. The side panel shows from 1200 px. Checked at 390, 700, 1000 and 1440 on all 16 boards: tab bar only at 390, side panel only at 1440.
Conversation’s Coming upFixShows the dates after today that the sidebar doesn’t show: the clinical bag on Sunday, clinical on Monday, the renal quiz on Thursday, and clinical on 2 November. Checked against the sidebar’s five items: no overlap.
No Start session on Weak spotsFixStart session shows only on the Study overview. Checked: absent on the quiz, results and Weak spots views at all widths.
Study in the sidebar during a quizFixIt goes back to the topics. Checked at 1440 (sidebar) and 390 (tab bar).
One main action on StudyFixEach objective’s Quiz me is a quiet row action, shown on hover or keyboard focus and hidden on the phone. Checked: 0 of 9 visible at rest; Start session is the only primary.
Week view at 600 to 1199 pxFixBelow 1200 px the week becomes one row per day, so titles don’t squeeze. Checked: the longest title is one line at 700 and 1000. “Open the task” now lines up with “Quiz me on it” (both start at x = 1129 at 1440).
Progress without one-off tilesFixThe three stats are property rows in a standard card, the same parts as the task and date panels.
Record › By E.V., task steps, the two + New checkboxes, Copy summaryCutAll four are off the boards.

Undo is the bring-back or take-off for a list edit, and the decline for a weak spot. A logged session is never listed. Checked: accepting Plan the rest adds two rows, and Undo on the take-off brings the clinical bag back.

Plan the rest, Snooze to tomorrowKeptUnchanged.
